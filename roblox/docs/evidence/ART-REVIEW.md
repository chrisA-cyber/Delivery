# Studio art review

The five before captures use the first production set. The earlier greybox-take.jpg is a separate reference for the original round. The reference paintings are in art/concepts/. The captures show actual Studio rendering. A scripted fixture supplied the performance.

| Moment | Observation in first pass | Change and second capture |
|---|---|---|
| Wide | Native cylinders collapsed the apron width. The host appeared outside the stage. Seat upholstery rendered as small balls. | Built a stadium footprint and used stretchable upholstery, softened the velvet material and added light shafts. stage-wide.jpg |
| Close take | The lower half of the bright dial competed with the performer's face. | Masked the lower dial, added a brass pedestal and shifted the lens off axis. stage-take.jpg |
| Reveal | The score shared the frame with the oversized bright dial. | Limited the visible dial and kept the snap ring at eye level. stage-reveal.jpg |
| Winner | The camera found an empty mic after the avatar returned to its seat. | Delayed reseating through the hero shot. The performer is visible in stage-winner.jpg. |
| Daily | The board was surrounded by disconnected curtains and stage rails. | Corrected the apron, filled the wing and kept the individual Daily card in the HUD. stage-daily.jpg |

The published asset budget, ten-avatar phone frame rate and human art acceptance remain open. The host, materials and theatre take their shapes from the concepts. The offline build has simpler geometry and lighting than the key art.

The Galaxy A16 layout simulation also changed the HUD. The original mobile controls obscured the two bottom corners and the action area clipped even a single button. Phone-before.jpg shows that issue. Phone-revised.jpg shows the same set with stock movement controls hidden and two rows of 44-pixel buttons fully visible.
