## From this branch to a public show

1. **Try the local place.** Open `roblox/Delivery.rbxl` and press Play. You arrive outdoors at the Signal Park streetcar stop facing the bandstand and can walk immediately. **Map** opens the district atlas. **Live show** offers **Play with sound pads**, **Perform a voice line** or **Join the audience**. The walkthrough below gives the exact controls for a first visit.
2. **Create an owner-controlled private test experience.** Publish a private test copy there, set the player cap to ten and give only the test group access. The private test universe keeps its real data separate from release. Local Studio uses an in-memory store even when Studio API access is allowed. Hold public access until the later checks pass.
3. **Set the permitted audience and communication features.** The owner completes Maturity and Compliance truthfully and makes region and age decisions. Enable microphone access and approve Chat & Voice Groups APIs under Communication if using the stage route. The place requests the Audio API and disables default voice. Test eligible friends, incompatible pairs, personal mute and block in Team Test or on private Roblox servers. A failed permission check should leave the route silent. Check current eligibility in [Roblox voice documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/voice-chat) and [voice group requirements](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/VoiceChatService#GetChatGroupsAsync).
4. **Set audio and host features.** Upload or grant this experience access to the eight original masters under `art/sound/`. Enter the asset IDs in `src/shared/SoundIds.luau`. Test stings, speech, stage level and the Echo cue using actual devices. The local place plays arranged stings and three distinct Foley instruments from Roblox's installed sample kit. The generated host selector starts off. Enable `HostText` in `src/shared/Features.luau` only when your experience is eligible and your questionnaire matches. On failure the show uses reviewed host lines and a visible cue.
5. **Configure cosmetics and social settings.** Enable free private servers if desired. Create the five cosmetic passes, decide prices and enter the IDs in `Wardrobe.luau`. The optional dated-playbill developer product uses `Programme.luau`. Use a private test to verify prompts, a receipt retry, grants, rejoin and restoration. ID zero keeps an item unavailable for sale. The performance, backstage and district badges appear in the game’s progress screen. Roblox account badges would require a separate BadgeService integration if you choose to offer them.
6. **Run the friends test.** Use [the 30-minute protocol](TEST-PLAN.md). Check persistent Daily records across two live servers, friend and troupe rewards, a working invite link and public-room matching in the private universe with appropriate test servers. Compare the observed funnel against Creator Analytics after its reporting delay. A physical mid-range phone with ten avatars and multiple real microphones is essential to the decision.
7. **Choose whether to launch.** Resolve any failed privacy, persistence, store, frame-rate or readability checks first. Review the supplied waterfront gameplay thumbnail alongside the illustrated theatre cover. Review the listing description, illustrated key art, icon and trailer plan in [the store handoff](STORE.md). Rebuild from the chosen revision. The owner controls the merge, Roblox publication, listing and opening public access.

## First visit on foot

Start walking immediately using normal Roblox controls. From the Signal Park gateway, walk ahead to the bandstand for Signal Run or turn left to the streetcar stop. Follow Lantern Street west through Broadcast Plaza to reach the waterfront. From the plaza, walk south around the rooftop terrace to reach the Night Market. **Map → Outdoor district** shows these routes and your location. **Theatre block** zooms in on the four studio storefronts. Select a landmark to read its activity, then use **Visit landmark** or **Visit room** to travel. The theatre and rooftop each offer walking and taking a seat. **Map → How to play → District guide** explains movement and the streetcar. **District records** shows your best buoy score, quickest park run and number of market deliveries.

| Stop | Try this | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Broadcast Plaza | Walk to **Marquee Relay**. Use **Turn dial** to match the three numbered bulbs to the ticket. The left switch changes left and middle. The middle switch changes middle and right. The right switch changes right. | A nearby friend can contribute to the same puzzle. |
| Lantern Street · streetcar | Approach a marked stop. Use **Board streetcar** as the car arrives. Choose **Request stop**, then **Get off** at the platform. | Ride from the plaza to the pier or the park without joining a stage round. |
| Waterfront Pier · west | Walk to the buoy crane, use **Work the buoy crane**, then **Reel in** on each light pulse. | The result shows the signals caught out of three. Each successful catch earns one stage XP. |
| Signal Park · east | At the bandstand use **Enter the signal circuit**. Cross the three marked arches in the ticket’s order, then return to the bandstand. | Look for a changing private waypoint, the timer and a finish time. Completion earns five crowd XP. |
| Night Market · south | Use **Use the wrapping counter** on foot, or choose **Take market order** after a map visit. Collect paper and ribbon from the specified stalls in order. Return to the counter and select the stamp on the ticket. | The order gives a parcel name and a Sun, Moon or Star stamp. A completed parcel earns four crowd XP. |
| Main Theatre | Choose **Live show → Join the audience**, or select **Watch show** on the map. Pick a direction, send a reaction, predict a score and cast a ballot. To perform, use **Perform**, choose a format and accept the fresh offer. | The cue card explains the current action and the timer shows how long it remains available. **Menu → Leave seat & explore** returns movement controls. |
| Studio B · west side | Choose **Visit room → Start rehearsal**, then tap the matching pad at **GO**. | Bell, Squeak and Thump should sound distinct. Two correct cues in one drill earn the Cobalt studio mic. |
| Writers’ Room · west side | Use **Change setup** and **Change ending**, then **Stamp this card**. Select **Perform this card** and accept your stage invitation. | Check the stamped line against the actual card on stage. |
| Props Warehouse · east side | Choose **Take an order**. Walk to each listed prop in order and use **Pack prop**. Return to the dispatch desk after collecting all three. | The ticket updates after each pickup. Delivery earns crowd XP and the Cherry cue stand. |

| Critics’ Balcony · rooftop | Choose **Explore rooftop**. At **Cast Portraits**, select **Pose for a portrait**, **Change framing**, then **Take photo**. The player chooses whether to save. To vote from the gallery, use **Watch show** on the map. | Returning to the venue should restore the walking camera. Three accurate predictions from a balcony seat earn Balcony Critic. |

For a first microphone test, choose **Live show → Perform a voice line → Use my microphone**. Speak normally during the check and read the line when **On air** appears. Studio also offers **Try a scripted take → Play scripted take** for a synthetic performance. Ask first-time players to choose their own route before showing them this walkthrough. Record where they hesitate, what they try first and which joke they volunteer to repeat.

## A walk through the town

From Signal Park, walk west on Lantern Street. Needle Drop Records is the first playable storefront on the park side. Dockside Post is on the pier side. Pick up a parcel at the postal counter and bring it to the address printed on the ticket. To play the listening game, use the record deck, listen to the labeled riff and repeat it on the three pads.

Follow the west pedestrian lane toward the Night Market for Copper Kettle Bakehouse. Use the counter and assemble the printed recipe in order. On the east lane, step inside Pocket Print Gallery to pick a skyline, ink and greeting. Your most recent postcard stays in the gallery. **Map → Town shops** shows all four addresses. Between Needle Drop and the market, the Clock Court footpath has three musical bell pulls and benches. Use the normal jump control to leave a bench.
