# Delivery store handoff

**Experience name**  
Delivery

**Description**  
Get a line. The room gives you a direction. You have ten seconds to make it land.

Explain your last obby reset like a breaking news bulletin. Whisper the worst tycoon update ever announced. Build a card from two terrible excuses and try to keep a straight face. Take the stage with an eligible mic, or grab a seat and direct the show, toss a rose, call the score and earn crowd rank. Bring friends for face-offs and try the same Daily card together.

**Five cosmetic passes**  
Gilded Roses changes a rose's colour. Blue Finale changes the performer's confetti. Violet Follow Spot recolours the stage key light. Rose Marquee recolours the stage meter. Curtain Glint adds an entrance burst. One look can be equipped. The server verifies ownership. The owner chooses IDs and prices in the published private test.
