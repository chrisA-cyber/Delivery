## District revision

| Choice | Reason | How to reverse it |
|---|---|---|
| Spawn at the Signal Park tram gateway facing the bandstand, with walking controls available immediately. Keep the live house show running while players work elsewhere. | Players see the park and boulevard first, can try a timed run nearby, and can reach the plaza or waterfront on the streetcar. Live show opens the role picker whenever a player wants a stage or audience turn. Brookhaven and Theme Park Tycoon 2 informed the connected-destination design. | Route arrival back to the theatre role card and remove the campus entrance. |
| Use an outdoor district atlas and a closer theatre-block view. Each shows the avatar’s location and selectable landmarks. Walking and taking a theatre or gallery seat are separate inputs. | Signs, numbered targets and activity prompts help a new player find a job without a long tutorial. A player can explore the rooftop while the show is live. | Hide quick travel and retain physical doorway and desk prompts, or restore the earlier theatre-only navigation. |
| Connect the waterfront, park and market by streets, with a streetcar on the east–west route. Give each destination a distinct job. | Players can explore without entering a building. A timing activity, a footrace and a two-stall shopping order make different use of the same walking controls. | Disable each public station and remove it from the district atlas. |
| Make Sound booth a complete three-beat performance, and place its private rehearsal in Studio B. | A player without a mic can perform an audible, scored act. Timing measures pad play, and the audience judges comedy on stage. Epic Minigames and Dress To Impress informed the short cycle and complementary performer/audience jobs. | Remove Sound booth from the format list and Studio B from the map. Keep the earlier no-mic crowd roles. |
| Put card stamping, an ordered prop run and a fitting mirror in separate rooms. | Each room gives the visitor a distinct activity and result. Reviewed writing presets preserve the original no-free-text rule. Earned looks carry backstage progress into the show. | Disable the workstation prompts and menu entries. Retain stamped-card and earned-look fields for older profiles. |
| Place a shared marquee puzzle at the entrance and an opt-in cast-photo station on the rooftop. | Friends can participate outside the performer queue. Numbered bulbs work without colour recognition. The photo station leaves gallery saving to the player. | Remove the two stations and stop issuing their in-game badges. |
| Pin Trove for microphone cleanup and Feather for cross-device interface symbols. | Reusing licensed, inspectable GitHub code and paths reduced custom work. The shipped place can open without contacting those repositories. | Replace the scoped cleanup and glyph helpers with native equivalents, then remove their imports and licence bundles. |
