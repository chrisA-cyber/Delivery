### Product description

Most voice apps want you to sound polished. Delivery wants you to commit.

Open the game and you immediately get two things: a line and an energy. The line might be a group-chat defense, a tiny villain monologue, or a completely normal sentence that has made a terrible career choice. The energy tells you how to perform it—like a defeated final boss, a suspicious weather alert, or an intern with leverage.

Record a take in the browser and Delivery returns an entertainment scorecard across Commitment, Comedy, Accuracy, and Chaos, plus a written verdict, a screenshot-ready “receipt,” and a Director's Note designed to tempt one better—or worse—attempt.

You can:

- play instantly without creating an account
- take the shared Daily Challenge
- stack rounds in Hot Streak
- try contradictory Impossible Energies
- send a specific line and direction to a friend
- publish a result, react to great disasters, and climb themed leaderboards
- use a clean Stream Mode for creator sessions

We built the content system as a living comedy desk: deep evergreen packs, scheduled daily prompts, expiring trend slots, and moderated community submissions. Recordings are private by default, sharing is explicit, and the joke is never allowed to target someone's identity.

Delivery is for friend groups, voice-note overachievers, streamers, and anyone who has ever thought, “I could say that with more conviction.”

Your first round is waiting. Dignity is optional.

## Maker first comment

Hey Product Hunt—we made Delivery because voice notes are already a performance format; they just lacked stage directions and consequences.

The first prototype was one button and a tiny set of prompts. What surprised us was that people did not chase a perfect score. They chased a more specific reaction: “Again, but this time like you know the ship is sinking and still need everyone to complete the survey.”

That became the product:

- instant play before signup
- a line plus an oddly precise energy
- fast browser recording and playback
- a structured entertainment scorecard
- a Director's Note that turns the result into the next round
- challenges and result cards that carry the joke outside the app

We care a lot about the boundaries too. Recordings start private. Publication is explicit and reversible. Community lines are reviewed. The judge evaluates the requested performance—not accent, identity, health, or similarity to a real person's voice.

Today we would love feedback on three things:

1. Did your first prompt make you want to perform immediately?
2. Did the verdict feel specific to your take?
3. Who did you challenge before closing the tab?

Drop your funniest receipt below. We will be playing all day.

## Product Hunt maker FAQ

### Is this a serious vocal assessment?

No. Delivery is an entertainment game. Its categories describe a performance against a playful prompt; they are not scientific measures of talent, personality, identity, or ability.

### Do I need an account?

Not for the first round. An account saves history, streaks, favorites, public profile choices, and friend challenges.

### What happens to my recording?

The launch policy should state the exact retention configuration. Product copy must emphasize that recordings begin private, are processed to create the result, and become shareable only after an explicit user choice. Users can unpublish and delete.

### Can I submit a line?

Yes. Submissions are screened and reviewed before they can enter play. A submission is not automatically public.

### Can it imitate a celebrity or streamer?

No. Delivery scores the supplied words and energy; it does not provide or encourage voice cloning or deceptive impersonation.

### What is Pro?

Replace with final verified facts before launch: unlimited or expanded play allowance, exclusive packs, advanced personal stats, custom challenges, and priority creator features. Pro does not buy higher scores or leaderboard advantage.

### Does it work on mobile?

Yes, on supported modern browsers with microphone permission. The real-device support matrix must be complete before making broader compatibility claims.
